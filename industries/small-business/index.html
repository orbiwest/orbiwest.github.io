Industry

Small Business IT

A practical IT foundation for organizations that need reliable systems without a large internal IT department.

Typical priorities

The exact approach depends on users, devices, applications, budget, risk and operational constraints.

  • Endpoint and user support
  • Microsoft 365 and cloud collaboration
  • Networks and Wi-Fi
  • Security baseline improvements
  • Backup and recovery planning
  • Vendor coordination

What we avoid

We do not assume every environment needs the same products, the same security stack or the same support model. The goal is a solution that can actually be operated and maintained.

Our approach

  • Understand workflows before changing systems
  • Keep documentation and supportability visible
  • Use security controls appropriate to the environment
  • Prioritize by operational impact
