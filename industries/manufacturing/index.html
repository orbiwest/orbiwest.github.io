Manufacturing & Operations IT

Manufacturing environments mix office IT, plant connectivity, shared workstations, specialized systems and connected operational technology.

What tends to matter in this environment

Manufacturing environments mix office IT, plant connectivity, shared workstations, specialized systems and connected operational technology.

Where Orbiwest can help

  • Plant and office switching, Wi-Fi and WAN
  • Segmented networks for business, facility and OT/IoT devices
  • Shared endpoints, kiosks and device management
  • Servers, virtualization, backup and recovery
  • Camera, access and facility-sensor networks
  • Vendor remote access and monitoring
  • Multi-site standardization and project rollouts

What a strong environment should look like

  • Keep plant and office traffic intentionally separated
  • Make vendor access controlled and traceable
  • Reduce single points of failure in network and recovery paths
  • Document dependencies before upgrades or production changes

How we can work with you

We coordinate the IT, network and security layers and involve appropriate industrial or controls specialists when the work moves beyond general IT responsibility.

Engagement models

  • Fully managed IT
  • Co-managed IT
  • Managed security
  • Assessment or roadmap
  • Project engineering
