Industry

Education IT

Technology support for schools and education environments where devices, classrooms, networks, accounts and testing all need to work together.

Technology areas we can support

  • Student and staff device support
  • Chromebooks and shared-device workflows
  • Classroom displays and interactive technology
  • Wi-Fi and network reliability
  • Printers and shared peripherals
  • Account and access assistance
  • Testing-day technical support
  • Camera/access-control technology by scope

Our approach

We start with how people actually use technology in the environment. Recommendations consider operational impact, supportability, security, available staff and budget rather than assuming every organization needs the same stack.

Priorities

  • Minimize classroom disruption
  • Keep shared technology supportable
  • Separate student/staff/guest/device networks appropriately
  • Document recurring systems and site dependencies
