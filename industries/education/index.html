Education IT

Schools need technology that works during ordinary instruction and during the days when failure has an immediate classroom impact.

What tends to matter in this environment

Schools need technology that works during ordinary instruction and during the days when failure has an immediate classroom impact.

Where Orbiwest can help

  • Student and staff endpoints, Chromebooks and shared devices
  • Classroom displays, interactive technology and printers
  • Wi-Fi, switching, filtering and segmented student/staff/guest networks
  • Identity, account access and Microsoft or Google environments
  • Testing-day readiness and rapid issue response
  • Cameras, access control and facility technology where included

What a strong environment should look like

  • Minimize classroom interruption
  • Keep shared devices and accounts supportable
  • Document classroom, network and testing dependencies
  • Give school staff clear escalation and repeatable SOPs

How we can work with you

We can provide ongoing school IT support, work beside an internal technology coordinator, or handle infrastructure and security projects without forcing the school into a single all-inclusive model.

Engagement models

  • Fully managed IT
  • Co-managed IT
  • Managed security
  • Assessment or roadmap
  • Project engineering
