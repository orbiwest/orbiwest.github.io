Commercial Real Estate & Property Technology

Commercial properties combine tenant-facing connectivity, building operations and physical-security systems that increasingly depend on IP networks and cloud management.

What tends to matter in this environment

Commercial properties combine tenant-facing connectivity, building operations and physical-security systems that increasingly depend on IP networks and cloud management.

Where Orbiwest can help

  • Property and management-office networks
  • Internet, Wi-Fi and segmented connected-device networks
  • Cameras, NVR/VMS, access control and intercom
  • Environmental sensors and smart-facility integrations
  • Conference-room and AV connectivity
  • Vendor coordination and infrastructure monitoring
  • Cybersecurity for connected building systems

What a strong environment should look like

  • Keep property technology separate from business-user networks where appropriate
  • Give property teams clear platform access and SOPs
  • Document network and facility dependencies
  • Coordinate technology vendors instead of leaving gaps between them

How we can work with you

We focus on the IT, network, cloud and integration layers and coordinate regulated electrical, life-safety or door-hardware work with qualified providers.

Engagement models

  • Co-managed IT
  • Managed security
  • Assessment or roadmap
  • Project engineering
