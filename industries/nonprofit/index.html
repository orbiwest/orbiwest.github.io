Industry

Nonprofit & Faith-Based Organization IT

Reliable, maintainable technology with attention to shared devices, staff access, volunteers, facilities and budget.

Technology priorities in this environment

Support should follow real workflows, not force every organization into the same stack.

Shared access without shared passwords

Use named accounts, groups and appropriate access wherever practical so staff changes do not leave uncontrolled credentials behind.

Budget-aware lifecycle

Prioritize aging devices, network equipment, backup and security improvements by operational risk rather than replacing everything at once.

Facilities and events

Support Wi-Fi, AV-adjacent connectivity, cameras/access systems and temporary event needs within a maintainable network design.

Staff and volunteer transitions

Make onboarding/offboarding and access review practical when roles change frequently or people serve seasonally.

Continuity

Protect important operational data and document recovery ownership so limited staff are not forced to improvise during an outage.

How Orbiwest approaches the environment

We look at users, devices, sites, identity, cloud services, network dependencies, vendors, security and recovery together. Recommendations are phased around operational impact, supportability, available staff and budget.

Scope stays explicit

Industry context informs the design, but regulated, code-governed or specialized requirements are handled only where the engagement and qualifications support them. When another specialist or licensed trade is required, the technical integration can be coordinated rather than misrepresented as in-house scope.

Priorities

  • Protect limited staff time
  • Keep shared technology understandable
  • Improve account/access hygiene
  • Prioritize work by impact and budget
