Solution

AI & Workflow Automation

Use automation or AI to remove repetitive work without losing control of data, approvals and exception handling.

Why organizations use this solution

This is useful when staff repeatedly copy information between systems, search large knowledge sets manually or spend time on predictable tasks that still need oversight.

What we examine

  • The current process and where time is lost
  • Data sources, permissions and privacy
  • APIs, Power Automate and integration options
  • Where AI adds value versus normal automation
  • Human approval and exception paths
  • Logging, support and ownership after launch

What the work should produce

A workflow that saves measurable effort, has clear boundaries and can be understood, paused or maintained by the customer team.

Customer handoff

Findings, decisions and important operating information are explained and documented. Your team can keep the work in-house, ask Orbiwest to manage it, or use a co-managed model.

Typical next step

  • Assessment and priorities
  • Remediation or migration project
  • Co-managed improvement
  • Ongoing managed service
