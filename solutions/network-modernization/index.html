Solution

Network Modernization

Improve reliability and supportability across switching, segmentation, Wi-Fi and firewall infrastructure.

What we look at

A structured way to clean up an aging or difficult network without replacing equipment simply because it is old.

  • Topology and dependency review
  • Switching, VLAN and addressing design
  • Wi-Fi coverage and capacity
  • Firewall, VPN and WAN dependencies
  • Redundancy and failover
  • Monitoring and documentation
  • Cutover and rollback planning

What you should get from the work

The goal is a network that is easier to understand, change and troubleshoot. Recommendations can be phased around risk, budget and lifecycle instead of forcing a single large replacement.

Customer handoff We explain the findings, provide the useful documentation, and make sure your team understands what it can operate directly and what Orbiwest can manage for you.
