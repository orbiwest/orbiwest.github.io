Business Continuity

Clarify what is protected, who owns recovery and what must come back first.

What we review

  • Map critical systems/dependencies
  • Review backup coverage
  • Clarify retention
  • Identify restore ownership
  • Define recovery priorities
  • Plan restore testing

Expected outcome

A clearer technical picture, prioritized next steps and fewer unknowns when the environment needs to change or recover.

Best fit

  • Recurring problems lack a clear root cause
  • The environment has grown without documentation
  • A major upgrade or migration is approaching
  • You want a roadmap before ongoing support

Everyday IT support, serious infrastructure work, cybersecurity, cloud, AI and connected systems—handled by people who explain what they are doing and why.
