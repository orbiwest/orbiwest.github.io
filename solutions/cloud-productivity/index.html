Solution

Cloud & Workplace Modernization

Make Microsoft 365, identity, devices and collaboration easier to administer and safer for users.

Why organizations use this solution

This is useful when Microsoft 365 has accumulated stale access, inconsistent MFA, confusing SharePoint/Teams permissions, licensing waste or manual workflows.

What we examine

  • Microsoft 365 licensing and administration
  • Entra ID, MFA and privileged roles
  • Teams, SharePoint and OneDrive
  • Intune and device enrollment where useful
  • Permissions, guest access and stale accounts
  • Workflow, Power Platform and backup opportunities

What the work should produce

Cleaner administration, fewer workarounds, clearer access and a documented plan for any migration or device-management changes that are actually needed.

Customer handoff

The handoff includes the information needed to understand what changed, what remains open and who owns the next step. Ongoing management is optional.

Typical next step

  • Assessment and priorities
  • Remediation or migration project
  • Co-managed improvement
  • Ongoing managed service
