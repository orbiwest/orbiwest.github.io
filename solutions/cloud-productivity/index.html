Solution

Cloud Productivity

Make Microsoft 365 and collaboration tools easier to administer and safer to use.

What we look at

Improve Microsoft 365, identity, collaboration and device workflows without turning every problem into a migration.

  • Microsoft 365 account and licensing review
  • Entra ID, MFA and sign-in experience
  • Teams, SharePoint and OneDrive
  • Device enrollment and Intune where appropriate
  • Permissions and stale access
  • Workflow and Power Platform opportunities
  • Cloud backup and recovery considerations

What you should get from the work

The outcome should be simpler administration and fewer workarounds for users. If a migration is needed, we document identity, data, DNS and user-impact dependencies before the cutover.

Customer handoff We explain the findings, provide the useful documentation, and make sure your team understands what it can operate directly and what Orbiwest can manage for you.
