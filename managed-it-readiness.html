Managed IT Readiness

Use this checklist to identify whether support is mostly reactive or becoming operationally mature.

On this pageInventoryMonitoringPatchingDocumentationSupport flow

Inventory

Do you know what devices, systems and subscriptions you own?

Monitoring

Can you identify unhealthy endpoints before users report them?

Patching

Is there an agreed patch cadence and exception process?

Documentation

Are critical credentials, diagrams and recovery procedures controlled and documented?

Support flow

Do users know where to report issues and can support history be tracked?

This guide is general operational guidance, not legal, compliance or vendor-specific professional advice. Adapt controls to your environment and requirements.
