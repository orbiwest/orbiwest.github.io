# Orbiwest Technologies — Web Brand Rules

## Logo
- Use the eagle/globe emblem fully visible.
- Never crop the orbital ring.
- Never place the emblem in a visible square or framed box.
- At normal header sizes, use the emblem plus the live ORBIWEST TECHNOLOGIES wordmark.
- At tiny sizes, use the emblem alone.

## Wordmark
- ORBI = Metallic Navy
- WEST = restrained Champagne Gold
- TECHNOLOGIES = spaced uppercase, dark navy/graphite
- Do not overuse shimmer, glow, bevels or reflections.

## Core colors
- Deep Navy: #061A33
- Metallic Navy: #0B2E5F
- Royal Blue: #165EAD
- Champagne Gold: #C9A24B
- Brushed Silver: #C7CDD3
- Graphite: #4D545C

## Brand personality
Professional, approachable, technically competent, secure, calm and dependable.

Avoid:
- cartoon technology graphics
- giant repeated logos
- neon effects
- fake dashboards
- unsupported statistics
- fake testimonials
- fake partner badges
- exaggerated marketing language

## Primary positioning
Managed IT · Networks · Cybersecurity · Cloud

## Primary headline
Technology You Can Rely On.
People You Can Reach.

## Brand promise
Secure · Connect · Empower
