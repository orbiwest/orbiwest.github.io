Accessibility

Accessibility at Orbiwest.com

We aim to make the public website usable with common browsers, keyboards, touch devices, zoom and assistive technology.

Design approach

The site uses semantic headings and landmarks, visible keyboard focus, responsive layouts, descriptive text links where practical, and color combinations chosen for readable contrast.

Keyboard and touch

Primary navigation, menus, links and calls to action are intended to work without a mouse. Interactive targets are spaced and sized for touch use, and the mobile menu can be dismissed with the Escape key.

Zoom, reflow and motion

Content is designed to reflow on narrow screens and when browser zoom is increased. The site also respects the operating system's reduced-motion preference for users who prefer less animation.

Third-party destinations

Links that leave Orbiwest.com may lead to services or websites that Orbiwest does not control. Their accessibility behavior can differ from this site.

Feedback

If something on Orbiwest.com is difficult to use, email info@orbiwest.com with the page URL, device/browser and a short description of the problem. We will use that information to evaluate the issue and improve the public site where practical.

Last updated: November 10, 2026.

Useful details to include

  • Page or feature affected
  • Browser and device
  • Keyboard or assistive technology if relevant
  • What you expected to happen
