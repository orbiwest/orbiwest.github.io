PASS

The website now has a single reader journey and one consistent service taxonomy. The approved visual design, logo, hero image and typography were preserved. This audit focused on wording, structure, reader comprehension and consistency.

## Reader journey
- Homepage: understand Orbiwest and the major capability areas without reading the whole catalog.
- Services: choose a technical capability and understand MSP versus MSSP in plain English.
- Solutions: start from a desired outcome when the exact technical service is not yet known.
- Industries: see how priorities change by operating environment.
- Experience: review representative technical depth without confusing platform names with certifications or partnerships.
- Resources: learn the concepts, prepare for a project and use the technology glossary.
- How We Work: understand customer ownership, access, documentation, SOPs and engagement models.
- Contact: explain the problem without needing to diagnose it first.

## Content principle
The site no longer tries to prove breadth by repeating every capability on every page. Breadth lives in the Service Directory and Technology Experience pages. Individual pages explain the reader's problem, what Orbiwest can take on, how the work is handled, what improves afterward and how ownership is handed back or managed.
