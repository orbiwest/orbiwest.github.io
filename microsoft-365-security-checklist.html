Microsoft 365 Security Checklist

Start with identity because compromised accounts can undermine otherwise good endpoint and network controls.

On this pageMFAAdmin rolesSign-in reviewRecoverySharing

MFA

Require MFA for privileged and user accounts where licensing and operational requirements allow.

Admin roles

Limit privileged roles and avoid using administrative accounts for daily work.

Sign-in review

Investigate unusual sign-ins, legacy authentication and stale accounts.

Recovery

Keep account recovery methods current and controlled.

Sharing

Review external sharing and guest access for collaboration services.

This guide is general operational guidance, not legal, compliance or vendor-specific professional advice. Adapt controls to your environment and requirements.
