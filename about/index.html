About Orbiwest

Good IT should be understandable, supportable and owned.

Orbiwest Technologies LLC is a Chicago-area technology services company focused on managed IT, cloud, cybersecurity, infrastructure, data protection, AI and automation, business systems, and connected facilities.

Why we built Orbiwest

Most IT problems do not stay inside one category. A sign-in issue may turn out to be identity. A slow application may be the WAN. A camera problem may be PoE, VLANs or storage. We built Orbiwest around that reality: own the problem, pull in the right person, and keep working it until the cause is clear.

How we want the work to feel

Clear communication. Practical troubleshooting. Changes that are supportable after the project is finished. Documentation that helps the next person understand what was done. Security considered during normal operations rather than added only after something goes wrong.

What we do not want to be

We do not want to sell a product simply because it is fashionable, publish made-up performance statistics, or pretend one technology stack fits every customer. Recommendations should make sense for the environment, risk, budget and people who must operate it.

Core principles

  • Own the problem, not just the ticket
  • Prefer maintainable solutions
  • Document meaningful changes
  • Protect access and recovery
  • Explain decisions clearly
  • Match technology to actual need
