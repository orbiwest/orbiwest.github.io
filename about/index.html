About Orbiwest

Good IT should leave the customer more confident and more in control.

Orbiwest Technologies LLC is a Chicago-area technology services company focused on managed IT, cloud, cybersecurity, infrastructure, data protection, AI and automation, business systems, and connected facilities.

Why we built Orbiwest

Most IT problems cross more than one system, but the customer should never lose visibility because the work is complicated. We built Orbiwest around two ideas: own the problem until it is clear, and keep the customer informed enough to understand and operate the environment afterward.

How we work with customers

We explain the plan before important changes. We tell you what we changed and what we verified. Authorized customer contacts keep the agreed access to customer owned systems. We document the environment and provide SOPs for routine work. If you want Orbiwest to manage everything, we can. If you want your own team to stay hands on, we make that easy too.

What we avoid

We do not want to hide the environment behind our tools, keep credentials hostage, or create dependency by withholding documentation. We also do not recommend technology simply because it is fashionable. The right solution should make sense for the environment, risk, budget and people who have to operate it.

Core principles

  • Customer first, not tool first
  • Explain important changes clearly
  • Document the environment and the work
  • Keep customer ownership and secure access clear
  • Provide SOPs and practical handoff
  • Manage fully or co manage, based on what the customer wants
