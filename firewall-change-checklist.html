Firewall Change Checklist

Firewall work should be treated as a controlled change because a small mistake can affect the whole site.

On this pageBefore the windowStage changesRollbackValidateCloseout

Before the window

Export a backup, document interfaces, policies, VPNs and current connectivity.

Stage changes

Prepare as much as possible offline. Confirm firmware, licensing and management access.

Rollback

Know exactly how to restore the old state before touching production cabling.

Validate

Test internet, DNS, routing, DHCP, VLANs, VPNs and critical applications.

Closeout

Save the final configuration and document what changed.

This guide is general operational guidance, not legal, compliance or vendor-specific professional advice. Adapt controls to your environment and requirements.
