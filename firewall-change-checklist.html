Firewall Change Checklist

A simple pre-change checklist for reducing avoidable downtime.

Before the change

Export configuration, document current state, confirm management access and identify rollback steps.

During the change

Change only what is required, preserve out-of-band access where possible and validate routing/DNS/DHCP dependencies.

After the change

Test internet, VPN, critical applications, internal routing and logging before closing the change window.

This resource is general operational guidance, not a substitute for environment-specific assessment or contractual requirements.
