# Content strategy

The site is arranged around the questions a prospective client is likely to ask:

1. What do you do?
2. Can you solve the kind of problem I have?
3. Why should I trust the way you work?
4. Do you understand my type of environment?
5. Can I engage you for a project instead of a long contract?
6. What technologies do you actually understand?
7. What happens if I contact you?
8. What common questions should be answered before I reach out?

No unsupported proof points have been added. Real testimonials, client logos, certifications, partner status or measurable service statistics should only be added later when they can be verified.
