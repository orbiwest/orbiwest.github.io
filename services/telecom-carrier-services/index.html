Connectivity

Telecom, Internet & Carrier Management

Keep circuits, carriers, voice services and site connectivity from becoming nobody's problem.

Fast when it can be. Resilient when it must be.

Where we get involved

  • ISP sourcing and coordination
  • Circuit turn up
  • Static IP and handoff planning
  • LTE or 5G backup
  • Voice and SIP services
  • Carrier escalation
  • Billing and contract review support
  • Site connectivity documentation

Common reasons clients call us

  • A new site needs service before opening
  • Carrier support keeps bouncing the issue back to IT
  • Internet failover is untested
  • Voice and data vendors do not share ownership

How the work is handled

We start with the current environment, the people who depend on it and the risk of changing it. The engineer responsible for the job maps the dependencies, agrees on the change path, validates the result and leaves behind the information needed for support.

We also leave the customer with the information needed to understand the network. That can include diagrams, addressing, VLANs, important dependencies, configuration backups and operating notes, depending on the platform and engagement.

Possible engagement

  • Assessment
  • Project
  • Managed service
  • Co managed support
  • Specialist engagement

Customer control

You keep control of the network.

We explain the design and changes in plain language. Authorized customer contacts retain the agreed administrative access, and we provide the information needed to understand and operate the environment.

Access stays with youAdministrator access is provided to authorized customer personnel using a secure access model.
Network documentationDiagrams, addressing, VLANs, important dependencies and material changes are documented.
Configuration handoffBackups, change notes and operating information are provided when the platform allows it.
SOPs for routine workWe document common tasks so your team can handle appropriate day to day operations without waiting on us.
You choose the operating model. If you want Orbiwest to fully manage the environment, we can take that responsibility. If you want your internal team to operate it, we provide access, documentation and SOPs so they can do that confidently. Co managed models can sit anywhere between those two points.

Tell us what is happening, what cannot go down, and what a good outcome looks like. We will help you decide the next step.
