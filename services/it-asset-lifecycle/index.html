Managed IT

IT Asset, Licensing & Device Lifecycle

Keep track of the hardware, software, warranties, subscriptions and replacement decisions that quietly become expensive when nobody owns them.

Less friction. More visibility. A calmer day for the people using the technology.

Where we get involved

Practical scope

  • Device inventory and ownership records
  • Warranty and lifecycle tracking
  • Software and subscription visibility
  • Hardware standards and replacement planning
  • Procurement coordination and device staging
  • Secure retirement / disposition coordination
  • License assignment and basic renewal planning

When this usually comes up

  • Laptops and peripherals are purchased ad hoc
  • Nobody can confidently say what is deployed or who owns it
  • Renewals arrive without enough time to review them
  • Old devices remain in management tools after they are retired

How we keep it supportable

Asset management does not need to become a giant CMDB project. We start with the information that is actually useful for support, security, budgeting and replacement decisions, then keep it maintainable.

We do not intentionally create lock in by withholding routine knowledge, credentials or documentation. Customers receive the access and SOPs appropriate to the environment and the agreed support model.

How we can engage

  • One-time assessment
  • Defined project
  • Co-managed support
  • Ongoing managed service
  • Vendor / platform coordination
