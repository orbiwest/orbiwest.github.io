IT Strategy, vCIO & Consulting

Technology planning and senior technical guidance for organizations that need a roadmap without a full internal IT leadership layer.

Clear scope. Careful change. Useful handoff.

When this helps

Where clients usually bring us in

  • Technology spending is reactive
  • Hardware and software lifecycle is unclear
  • Projects are chosen without a roadmap
  • Vendors drive decisions instead of business needs
  • Risk and technical debt are difficult to prioritize
  • Leadership needs a clearer view of upcoming technology work

What we can take on

  • Technology roadmap
  • Lifecycle and budget planning
  • Vendor evaluation
  • Risk and dependency review
  • Project planning
  • Standardization recommendations
  • Periodic technology reviews by agreement
  • Business-aligned recommendations

How we handle the work

Before changing anything, we look at the dependencies and the operational impact. That usually prevents a small technical fix from turning into a larger outage.

What we leave behind

Material configurations, decisions, dependencies and handoff information are documented as appropriate to the engagement so future support does not depend only on memory.

We explain the work, document the parts that matter and keep customer ownership clear. The goal is a system your team can understand and operate, whether or not Orbiwest remains involved.

Useful when you need

  • Clear technical ownership
  • Practical troubleshooting
  • Maintainable configuration
  • Better documentation
  • A realistic next step

Customer control
