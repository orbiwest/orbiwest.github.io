Microsoft Copilot Readiness, Deployment & Adoption

Prepare Microsoft 365 data, permissions and users before Copilot is turned on, then help people use it safely and productively.

Typical work

Practical scope

  • Copilot readiness assessment
  • License and user-group planning
  • SharePoint / OneDrive permission review
  • Data governance and oversharing review
  • Pilot-user selection
  • Prompt and workflow guidance
  • Adoption support and training
  • Usage, risk and value review when included in the engagement

Common reasons clients bring us in

  • Leadership wants Copilot but SharePoint permissions are messy
  • Users are experimenting with consumer AI tools
  • The organization wants a controlled pilot before wider licensing
  • Teams need examples tied to their actual work

How we work through it

Copilot can only respect the permissions and data model already in Microsoft 365. We focus on that foundation first, then adoption. Buying licenses before fixing access and content sprawl usually creates avoidable risk.

The workflow, integrations, permissions and approval points are documented. Customer data and business rules remain under customer control, and sensitive actions can require human approval.
