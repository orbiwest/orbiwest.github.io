Identity, Email & User Security

Authentication, account lifecycle and email-security controls focused on reducing common user and identity risk.

When this helps

Common reasons organizations call us

  • MFA coverage is inconsistent
  • Old accounts remain active
  • Administrator roles are not reviewed
  • Phishing and malicious email are recurring concerns
  • Offboarding does not reliably remove access
  • Third-party applications retain unnecessary permissions

What the service can include

  • MFA and identity hygiene
  • Privileged-access review
  • Account lifecycle and offboarding
  • Email-security configuration by scope
  • Anti-phishing controls by scope
  • Conditional access review by scope
  • SSO/access integration by scope
  • Third-party access review

How Orbiwest approaches the work

We first understand the current environment, business impact, ownership and dependencies. Changes are scoped around what is actually needed, with attention to supportability, security, documentation and recovery.

What we document

Material configurations, decisions, dependencies and handoff information are documented as appropriate to the engagement so future support does not depend only on memory.
