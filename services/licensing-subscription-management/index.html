Licensing & Subscription Management

Keep software, cloud and security licensing understandable before renewals turn into surprises.

Where we get involved

  • Microsoft licensing review
  • Security subscription review
  • RMM and backup licensing
  • Cloud subscription ownership
  • Renewal calendar
  • License assignment cleanup
  • Vendor quote comparison
  • Growth and capacity planning

Common reasons clients call us

  • Renewals arrive without enough notice
  • Licenses are assigned to inactive users
  • Different teams buy overlapping tools
  • Nobody owns the software renewal calendar

How the work is handled

We start with the current environment, the people who depend on it and the risk of changing it. The engineer responsible for the job maps the dependencies, agrees on the change path, validates the result and leaves behind the information needed for support.

We do not intentionally create lock in by withholding routine knowledge, credentials or documentation. Customers receive the access and SOPs appropriate to the environment and the agreed support model.

Customer control

Use us as much or as little as you need.

Some customers want Orbiwest to run the environment. Others want their own team to stay hands on. We support both models. The customer keeps ownership, visibility and the ability to operate independently.

Customer owned accountsCore business accounts, tenants and systems remain under customer ownership.
Shared visibilityAuthorized contacts can see the systems, documentation and service information relevant to their environment.
SOPs for common tasksWe document repeatable tasks so your staff can handle the work they want to own.
No unnecessary dependencyWe do not intentionally hide credentials, documentation or routine knowledge to create lock in.
You choose the operating model. If you want Orbiwest to fully manage the environment, we can take that responsibility. If you want your internal team to operate it, we provide access, documentation and SOPs so they can do that confidently. Co managed models can sit anywhere between those two points.
