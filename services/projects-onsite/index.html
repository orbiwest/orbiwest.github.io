Projects & On-Site Support

Hands-on technology work for migrations, replacements, installations, cutovers and physical troubleshooting.

When this helps

Common reasons organizations call us

  • A change requires someone on site
  • A firewall or network replacement has a fixed maintenance window
  • New hardware must be deployed and validated
  • A site needs technology cleanup or documentation
  • A migration has multiple vendors or dependencies
  • Remote troubleshooting is not enough

What the service can include

  • On-site troubleshooting
  • Firewall and network replacements
  • Device and infrastructure installations
  • Migrations and cutovers
  • Site surveys and documentation
  • Technology refresh projects
  • Vendor coordination
  • Post-change validation

How Orbiwest approaches the work

The first job is to understand what the business is relying on. From there we can decide whether the right answer is a repair, cleanup, migration or replacement.

What we document

Material configurations, decisions, dependencies and handoff information are documented as appropriate to the engagement so future support does not depend only on memory.

Clients usually call us for

  • Clear technical ownership
  • Practical troubleshooting
  • Maintainable configuration
  • Better documentation
  • A realistic next step
