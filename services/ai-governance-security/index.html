AI Governance, Security & Shadow-AI Controls

Create practical guardrails for AI use so employees can get value from the tools without quietly exposing sensitive business or customer data.

What this covers

Practical scope

  • AI acceptable-use and data-handling guidance
  • Approved-tool and account strategy
  • Shadow-AI discovery and risk discussion by scope
  • Identity, permissions and data-access review
  • Prompt / output handling guidance
  • Human-in-the-loop controls
  • Logging and audit considerations
  • Vendor / model risk review by scope

When it is worth looking at

  • Employees already use public AI tools with work data
  • Different teams are buying separate AI subscriptions
  • Copilot or agents will access sensitive information
  • Leadership wants a policy that is usable, not just restrictive

How we approach it

AI governance should make safe work easier, not push people toward hidden workarounds. We define what data can be used, what tools are approved, where human approval is required, and who owns exceptions.

How we can engage

  • Co-managed support
  • Ongoing managed service
  • Vendor / platform coordination
