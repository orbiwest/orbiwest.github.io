New Office, Move & Site Technology

Plan and coordinate the IT work behind a new office, relocation, expansion or site refresh.

When this helps

Common reasons organizations call us

  • A new location needs internet, network, Wi-Fi and user readiness
  • An office move has multiple technology vendors
  • A site needs cameras/access control along with IT infrastructure
  • Existing equipment must be relocated or replaced
  • Cutover timing needs to minimize business disruption
  • Leadership needs one technical plan across network, devices, cloud and vendors

What the service can include

  • Site technology discovery and requirements
  • ISP/carrier coordination
  • Network, firewall, switching and Wi-Fi planning
  • User/device move planning
  • Server/network equipment relocation by scope
  • Microsoft 365/cloud readiness
  • Cameras, NVR and access-control coordination
  • Printer/phone/VoIP coordination
  • Vendor scheduling and dependency tracking
  • Cutover and rollback planning
  • Post-move validation
  • Updated site documentation

How Orbiwest approaches the work

We map the current environment, dependencies, ownership and business impact before making changes. The goal is a supportable result with realistic sequencing, security, documentation and rollback where appropriate.

What is documented

Material configurations, decisions, dependencies, handoff information and agreed operating responsibilities are documented at a level appropriate to the engagement.

Good fit when you need

  • Clear technical ownership
  • Practical planning before change
  • Maintainable implementation
  • Security considered during design
  • Documentation and handoff

Frequently asked questions

Useful scope questions before a project begins.

Can you coordinate with cabling, ISP and security vendors?

Yes. Orbiwest can coordinate the technology dependencies and work with qualified trades/vendors for regulated or specialized physical work.

Can you reuse existing equipment?

Where practical and supportable, yes. Existing hardware should be evaluated for compatibility, lifecycle and risk.

Can cameras and door access be included in the same project?

Yes, by scope, while code-regulated electrical/door work may require qualified installers.
