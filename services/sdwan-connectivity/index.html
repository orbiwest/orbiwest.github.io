Networks

Internet, WAN, SD-WAN & Site Connectivity

Design and troubleshoot the links that connect offices, cloud services, remote users and critical applications.

What we handle

Practical scope

  • ISP and circuit planning
  • WAN routing and failover
  • SD-WAN evaluation and implementation when included in the engagement
  • Site-to-site VPN
  • LTE / 5G backup connectivity
  • QoS and application-path review
  • Multi-site connectivity documentation
  • Carrier escalation and coordination

When to call us

  • Multiple sites depend on inconsistent ISP setups
  • Failover exists but nobody trusts it
  • Voice or cloud traffic performs poorly
  • A new site needs internet and network connectivity before opening

How we handle the work

Connectivity projects fail when the carrier, firewall, switching and application teams each see only their piece. We document the end-to-end path and assign ownership before the cutover.

We also leave the customer with the information needed to understand the network. That can include diagrams, addressing, VLANs, important dependencies, configuration backups and operating notes, depending on the platform and engagement.

How we can engage

  • One-time assessment
  • Defined project
  • Co-managed support
  • Ongoing managed service
  • Vendor / platform coordination
