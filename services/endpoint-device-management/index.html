Endpoint & Device Management

Ongoing device maintenance and visibility across business and education endpoints.

What this can include

  • RMM operations by scope
  • Patch scheduling and follow-up
  • Endpoint health checks
  • Software and service troubleshooting
  • Basic asset visibility
  • Device onboarding and cleanup

How we approach it

We start with the current environment and the business impact. The goal is to improve reliability and supportability without creating unnecessary complexity.

How scope is set

Final scope depends on access, vendors, licensing, risk, timing, physical location and the agreed service arrangement or project statement.

What we aim to improve

  • More consistent endpoint maintenance
  • Fewer unmanaged devices
  • Better patch visibility
  • Cleaner device lifecycle operations
