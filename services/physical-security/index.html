Door hardware, life-safety interfaces, fire-alarm systems, permitting, electrical work and code-regulated installation may require licensed/qualified trades or vendors. Orbiwest can coordinate the IT/network/integration portion by scope. AI, biometric and license-plate capabilities also depend on platform support and applicable requirements.

Common integration patterns

  • Door access event linked to the nearby camera view
  • Intercom call with live video before releasing a door
  • AI person/vehicle event generating a supported alert or workflow
  • Camera and access systems on dedicated security VLANs
  • Remote viewing across several buildings or locations
  • Central user/credential administration where the platform supports it
  • NVR/VMS storage sized around retention and camera bitrate requirements
  • Health monitoring for cameras, recorders and supporting network infrastructure

Security and privacy are part of the design

Video and access systems contain sensitive operational data. Credentials, administrator rights, retention, remote access, firmware, network exposure and AI features should be configured deliberately and documented.
