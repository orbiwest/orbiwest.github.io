M&A Technology Due Diligence & Integration

Understand the technology risk, cost and integration work behind an acquisition, merger or new portfolio company before surprises appear after close.

What this usually includes

Practical scope

  • High-level technology inventory and dependency review
  • Identity, tenant and domain considerations
  • Cybersecurity and backup gap review
  • Licensing and vendor-contract visibility
  • Network, server and endpoint standardization needs
  • Technical debt and lifecycle risks
  • Day-1 / Day-30 integration planning
  • Separation / consolidation planning when included in the engagement

Where this becomes a problem

  • An acquisition target has limited IT documentation
  • Two Microsoft 365 tenants or networks must be combined
  • Leadership needs a realistic view of post-close technology work
  • Security gaps or unsupported infrastructure could affect integration cost

How we run the job

We focus on the operational questions that affect the deal: what systems are critical, what is unsupported, what identities and domains are involved, what contracts matter, what security gaps exist, and what must happen on day one versus later.

How we can engage

  • One-time assessment
  • Defined project
  • Co-managed support
  • Ongoing managed service
  • Vendor / platform coordination
