Conference Rooms, AV & Collaboration Spaces

Make meeting spaces predictable for the people who use them and supportable for the team behind them.

Where we get involved

  • Teams Rooms and Zoom Rooms
  • Displays and presentation systems
  • Room audio and microphones
  • Cameras
  • Wireless presentation
  • Room scheduling panels
  • Network and VLAN requirements
  • AV vendor coordination
  • Digital signage when included

Common reasons clients call us

  • Every meeting starts with troubleshooting
  • Rooms use different hardware and workflows
  • AV problems are actually network or account problems
  • A new office needs standardized meeting rooms

How the work is handled

We start with the current environment, the people who depend on it and the risk of changing it. The engineer responsible for the job maps the dependencies, agrees on the change path, validates the result and leaves behind the information needed for support.

We provide the agreed customer access to the relevant camera, access control, intercom or sensor platforms and document the network, devices and routine administration procedures that matter.
