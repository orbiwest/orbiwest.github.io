Managed Service Provider

MSP Services That Keep the Business Running

Orbiwest can run day-to-day IT, work alongside an internal team, or manage selected parts of the environment. You choose how much responsibility you want us to take. In every model, your organization keeps ownership, visibility and the ability to operate its own systems.

Managed IT is more than a help desk

A good MSP should make the customer less dependent on tribal knowledge, not more dependent on the MSP. We support users and systems, but we also explain the environment, document it, provide SOPs and keep customer access clear. If you want us to own the daily operations, we do. If your team wants to stay hands on, we work that way too.

Day-to-day operations

Help Desk & User Support

Remote support, account problems, applications, printers, shared devices, onboarding and everyday troubleshooting.

Endpoint Management

RMM, patching, software, health checks, device policy, lifecycle and remote remediation.

Microsoft 365 & Identity

Users, licensing, Teams, SharePoint, OneDrive, Entra ID, MFA and day-to-day cloud administration.

Network Operations

LAN, WAN, Wi-Fi, switches, firewalls, VPN, circuits, monitoring and vendor escalation.

Server & Cloud Operations

Windows, Linux, virtualization, storage, Azure, AWS, Google Cloud and supported hybrid environments.

Backup & Recovery

Backup monitoring, retention, restore readiness, BCDR, SaaS backup and recovery planning.

Ways clients use us

  • Fully managed IT for organizations that want one accountable technology team.
  • Co-managed IT for companies that already have internal IT and need more capacity or deeper engineering.
  • Project work for migrations, refreshes, office moves, network changes and other defined initiatives.
  • On-site support where remote troubleshooting is not enough.
  • vCIO and planning for lifecycle, budget, standards and technology roadmaps.
