AI Strategy, Readiness & Integration

Use AI where it has a clear job to do. Orbiwest helps identify useful use cases, prepare the data and access model, integrate the tools, and keep people in control of important decisions.

What this usually includes

What we can take on

  • AI readiness and use-case discovery
  • Microsoft Copilot and business-AI planning
  • Private AI assistants and retrieval-augmented generation (RAG) when included in the engagement
  • AI agents and multi-step workflow automation
  • Document classification, extraction and routing
  • CRM / ERP / Microsoft 365 / service-system integration
  • AI security, permissions and data-access review
  • Pilot design, testing, adoption and ongoing improvement

When clients bring us in

  • Staff are already using public AI tools without a policy
  • Leadership wants AI but has not identified a useful first project
  • Teams spend too much time copying information between systems
  • An internal knowledge base is difficult to search
  • A customer-facing or internal workflow could benefit from an assistant or agent

How we handle the work

We do not start by forcing AI into a process. First we ask what the work is, what data it touches, what errors would cost, and where a human still needs to approve the result. Sometimes the right answer is AI; sometimes a normal automation is safer and cheaper.

The workflow, integrations, permissions and approval points are documented. Customer data and business rules remain under customer control, and sensitive actions can require human approval.

Ways to work with us

  • Defined project
  • Co-managed support
  • Ongoing managed service
  • Vendor / platform coordination
