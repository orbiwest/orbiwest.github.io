Service

Cybersecurity

Practical security controls for identity, endpoints, networks, email and recovery, integrated into normal IT operations.

When this helps

When clients bring us in

  • MFA coverage is incomplete
  • Administrator access is broader than necessary
  • Endpoints are not consistently protected or patched
  • Security alerts do not have a clear owner
  • Email and account risks are increasing
  • Backup and recovery assumptions have not been tested

What we can take on

  • Identity and MFA hygiene
  • Endpoint security / EDR coordination
  • Privileged-access review
  • Patch and configuration hygiene
  • Firewall and segmentation review
  • Email-security controls when included in the engagement
  • DNS/web filtering when included in the engagement
  • Backup and recovery readiness
  • MDR/SOC/SIEM integration when included in the engagement
  • Vulnerability remediation tracking

How we handle the work

We review security as an operating process: what is protected, what is monitored, who receives alerts, what can be contained quickly and what still requires customer approval.

What you receive

We document control ownership, important exceptions, alert routing, response authority and the settings that matter for later review or handoff.

Security responsibilities are documented. The customer should know what is monitored, which actions Orbiwest can take, which actions require approval, and how to access the relevant reporting and tools.

A good fit when

  • Clear technical ownership
  • Practical troubleshooting
  • Maintainable configuration
  • Better documentation
  • A realistic next step
