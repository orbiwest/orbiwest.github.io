Service

Cybersecurity

Practical security controls for identity, endpoints, networks, email and recovery—integrated into normal IT operations.

When this helps

Common reasons organizations call us

  • MFA coverage is incomplete
  • Administrator access is broader than necessary
  • Endpoints are not consistently protected or patched
  • Security alerts do not have a clear owner
  • Email and account risks are increasing
  • Backup and recovery assumptions have not been tested

What the service can include

  • Identity and MFA hygiene
  • Endpoint security / EDR coordination
  • Privileged-access review
  • Patch and configuration hygiene
  • Firewall and segmentation review
  • Email-security controls by scope
  • DNS/web filtering by scope
  • Backup and recovery readiness
  • MDR/SOC/SIEM integration by scope
  • Vulnerability remediation tracking

How Orbiwest approaches the work

We first understand the current environment, business impact, ownership and dependencies. Changes are scoped around what is actually needed, with attention to supportability, security, documentation and recovery.

What we document

Material configurations, decisions, dependencies and handoff information are documented as appropriate to the engagement so future support does not depend only on memory.

Good fit when you need

  • Clear technical ownership
  • Practical troubleshooting
  • Maintainable configuration
  • Better documentation
  • A realistic next step
