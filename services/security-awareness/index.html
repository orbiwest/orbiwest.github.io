Security Awareness & Phishing Readiness

Help users recognize common attacks and give the organization a repeatable way to improve security behavior over time.

Where we get involved

Practical scope

  • Security-awareness platform coordination
  • Phishing simulation programs when included in the engagement
  • Training enrollment and completion tracking
  • New-user security orientation
  • Role-based topics for higher-risk users
  • Reporting and follow-up
  • Policy and reporting-channel guidance

When this usually comes up

  • Users keep seeing convincing phishing messages
  • Training is annual but nobody knows whether behavior is improving
  • Cyber insurance or customer requirements ask for awareness evidence
  • New hires receive little guidance on account and data security

How we keep it supportable

The goal is not to embarrass people who click. Training works better when it is short, relevant and tied to the real attacks users are seeing. We focus on habits and a clear reporting path.

How we can engage

  • One-time assessment
  • Defined project
  • Co-managed support
  • Ongoing managed service
  • Vendor / platform coordination
