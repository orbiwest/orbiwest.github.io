Service

Networks & Wireless

Switching, VLANs, Wi-Fi, DHCP, routing and network troubleshooting designed for dependable and supportable connectivity.

When this helps

Common reasons organizations call us

  • Wi-Fi coverage or roaming is inconsistent
  • VLANs and DHCP scopes are poorly documented
  • Switching changes are risky because dependencies are unclear
  • Remote sites or uplinks are unreliable
  • Devices end up on the wrong network
  • Troubleshooting depends on tribal knowledge

What the service can include

  • Network discovery and documentation
  • Switching and VLAN configuration
  • Wi-Fi troubleshooting and optimization
  • DHCP and IP-address planning
  • Routing and connectivity troubleshooting
  • PoE and camera/access-control network planning
  • Uplink and basic redundancy review
  • Monitoring integration when included in the engagement

How Orbiwest approaches the work

We avoid one-size-fits-all designs. The existing environment, the people supporting it, the risk and the budget all matter before we recommend a change.

What we document

Material configurations, decisions, dependencies and handoff information are documented as appropriate to the engagement so future support does not depend only on memory.

A strong fit for

  • Clear technical ownership
  • Practical troubleshooting
  • Maintainable configuration
  • Better documentation
  • A realistic next step
