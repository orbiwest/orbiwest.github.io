Visitor, Gate, Parking & LPR Workflows

Coordinate entry technology for visitors, vehicles and controlled areas, with video and access events connected where the platform supports it.

Where we get involved

Practical scope

  • Visitor-entry workflow design
  • Video intercom and remote entry
  • Gate / parking access integration
  • Temporary credentials and schedules
  • License-plate recognition workflows where supported and appropriate
  • Camera association with entry events
  • Notifications and audit trails
  • Multi-site administration by platform

When this usually comes up

  • Visitors wait for someone to physically open a door
  • Parking or gate access is separate from the rest of the security system
  • Vehicle events need video context
  • Temporary access is difficult to issue and revoke

How we keep it supportable

Entry workflows touch security, privacy and physical hardware. We design the IT, network, application and integration pieces carefully and coordinate regulated door/gate/electrical work with qualified providers where required.

How we can engage

  • One-time assessment
  • Defined project
  • Co-managed support
  • Ongoing managed service
  • Vendor / platform coordination
