Cloud Cost, Governance & Optimization

Put structure around cloud spend, access, standards and resource ownership before small decisions turn into recurring waste.

Where we get involved

What we can take on

  • Cloud inventory and ownership review
  • Rightsizing and unused-resource review
  • Basic budget, tagging and cost-allocation practices
  • Identity and privileged-access review
  • Backup, retention and lifecycle review
  • Configuration and security hygiene
  • FinOps-style reporting and recommendations when included in the engagement

When clients bring us in

  • Azure or cloud spend keeps growing without a clear reason
  • Resources were created for projects that have ended
  • Nobody is sure which team owns a subscription or workload
  • Cloud security and backup standards vary from system to system

How we handle the work

We look for the operational causes behind cloud waste: unclear ownership, inconsistent standards, over-provisioning, stale resources and poor visibility. The goal is not simply a cheaper bill; it is a cloud environment that is easier to explain and manage.

The customer remains the owner of its tenant, domains and data. We document the migration and administration model so authorized customer staff can manage, audit or transition the environment without depending on undocumented knowledge.

Ways to work with us

  • One-time assessment
  • Defined project
  • Co-managed support
  • Ongoing managed service
  • Vendor / platform coordination
