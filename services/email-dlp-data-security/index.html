Email, DLP & Data Security

Protect email and sensitive business data across Microsoft 365, cloud applications and endpoints.

Reduce noise. Clarify risk. Know who acts when it matters.

What is included

  • Email threat protection and anti-phishing
  • DMARC / SPF / DKIM
  • Account-takeover protection
  • Data Loss Prevention (DLP)
  • Data classification and retention when included in the engagement
  • Insider-risk tooling when included in the engagement
  • Cloud/SaaS data security
  • Encryption / secure sharing
  • Security awareness integration

What usually triggers the project

  • BEC and phishing are recurring concerns
  • Sensitive data leaves through email or cloud apps
  • Retention and DLP policies are inconsistent
  • Leadership wants clearer controls around AI and sensitive data

What we do differently

We keep the job practical: understand the current state, identify the real risk, make the smallest sound change, verify it, and document what the team needs to know next time.

Security responsibilities are documented. The customer should know what is monitored, which actions Orbiwest can take, which actions require approval, and how to access the relevant reporting and tools.

  • Assessment
  • Architecture / design
  • Migration / implementation
  • Troubleshooting

Customer control

Security should increase control, not take it away.

You should know what is being monitored, what happens when an alert fires, who can make changes, and what authority Orbiwest has during an incident. We document those responsibilities instead of hiding them behind a security dashboard.

VisibilityAuthorized customer contacts receive appropriate access to the security platforms and reporting used for their environment.
Response authorityEscalation, containment and approval responsibilities are agreed in advance.
Documented controlsImportant policies, exceptions, integrations and response procedures are recorded.
SOPs and runbooksWe provide practical operating and incident procedures that your team can use independently.
You choose the operating model. If you want Orbiwest to fully manage the environment, we can take that responsibility. If you want your internal team to operate it, we provide access, documentation and SOPs so they can do that confidently. Co managed models can sit anywhere between those two points.
