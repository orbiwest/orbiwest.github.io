SharePoint, Power Platform & Productivity Automation

Improve document, approval and collaboration workflows with tools your Microsoft 365 users already have.

What we handle

Practical scope

  • SharePoint site and document-library planning
  • Permissions, sharing and governance cleanup
  • Power Automate workflow design
  • Power Apps forms and lightweight applications when included in the engagement
  • Power BI dashboards and reporting when included in the engagement

When to call us
