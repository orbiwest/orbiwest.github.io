VoIP & Unified Communications

Voice, collaboration and network-readiness support for business communication platforms.

When this helps

When clients bring us in

  • Voice quality is inconsistent
  • Phones are on the wrong VLAN or QoS is unclear
  • Call routing changes are difficult
  • Remote users have communication problems
  • A phone-system migration is being considered
  • Voice vendor troubleshooting needs network context

What we can take on

  • VoIP network-readiness review
  • Voice VLAN and QoS review
  • Phone/user provisioning when included in the engagement
  • Call-flow troubleshooting
  • Microsoft Teams collaboration support
  • Vendor coordination
  • SIP/voice troubleshooting when included in the engagement
  • Migration planning

How we handle the work

We start with the current state, the people who depend on it, the important dependencies and the risk of change. The work is then scoped around a clear outcome, validation and a usable handoff.

What you receive

We document the decisions, dependencies and handoff information needed to support the work later.

We explain the work, document the parts that matter and keep customer ownership clear. The goal is a system your team can understand and operate, whether or not Orbiwest remains involved.

When clients bring us in

  • Clear technical ownership
  • Practical troubleshooting
  • Maintainable configuration
  • Better documentation
  • A realistic next step
