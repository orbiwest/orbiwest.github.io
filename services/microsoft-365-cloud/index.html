Microsoft 365 & Cloud

Microsoft 365, identity, collaboration and cloud administration that keeps access understandable and supportable.

When this helps

Common reasons organizations call us

  • User and admin roles are inconsistent
  • MFA or sign-in problems affect productivity
  • Teams/SharePoint usage has become confusing
  • Licensing is difficult to understand
  • Old accounts or third-party access remain active
  • A migration or tenant cleanup is needed

What the service can include

  • Microsoft 365 user and license administration
  • MFA and sign-in support
  • Entra ID / identity support by scope
  • Teams and SharePoint support
  • Mailbox and collaboration troubleshooting
  • Admin-role and access review
  • Google Workspace support by scope
  • Migration-readiness planning

How Orbiwest approaches the work

We first understand the current environment, business impact, ownership and dependencies. Changes are scoped around what is actually needed, with attention to supportability, security, documentation and recovery.

What we document

Material configurations, decisions, dependencies and handoff information are documented as appropriate to the engagement so future support does not depend only on memory.
