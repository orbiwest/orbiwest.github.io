Microsoft 365 & Cloud

Microsoft 365, identity, collaboration and cloud administration that keeps access understandable and supportable.

Move carefully. Modernize deliberately. Keep ownership clear.

When this helps

Where cloud work gets complicated

  • User and admin roles are inconsistent
  • MFA or sign-in problems affect productivity
  • Teams/SharePoint usage has become confusing
  • Licensing is difficult to understand
  • Old accounts or third-party access remain active
  • A migration or tenant cleanup is needed

What we can plan, move and manage

  • Microsoft 365 user and license administration
  • MFA and sign-in support
  • Entra ID / identity support when included in the engagement
  • Teams and SharePoint support
  • Mailbox and collaboration troubleshooting
  • Admin-role and access review
  • Google Workspace support when included in the engagement
  • Migration-readiness planning

How we move without losing the operating model

We keep the technical work tied to the real problem. That means checking the surrounding systems, planning the change and leaving enough documentation for future support.

What we leave behind

Material configurations, decisions, dependencies and handoff information are documented as appropriate to the engagement so future support does not depend only on memory.

The customer remains the owner of its tenant, domains and data. We document the migration and administration model so authorized customer staff can manage, audit or transition the environment without depending on undocumented knowledge.

Where this helps most

  • Clear technical ownership
  • Practical troubleshooting
  • Maintainable configuration
  • Better documentation
  • A realistic next step

Cloud work should not make a customer dependent on the consultant who performed the migration. We keep ownership, administrative access and documentation clear from the start.

Customer owned tenantsSubscriptions, domains and tenants remain under customer ownership and approved customer administration.
Admin accessAuthorized customer contacts keep the access needed to manage or audit the environment.
Migration recordsCutover decisions, dependencies, DNS, identity and important configuration changes are documented.
Operating SOPsWe provide practical procedures for common administrative work and recovery tasks.
You choose the operating model. If you want Orbiwest to fully manage the environment, we can take that responsibility. If you want your internal team to operate it, we provide access, documentation and SOPs so they can do that confidently. Co managed models can sit anywhere between those two points.
