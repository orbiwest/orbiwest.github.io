NOC, Monitoring & Observability

Monitor networks, servers, services and user experience so the team can act before every issue becomes a user complaint.

Less friction. More visibility. A calmer day for the people using the technology.

Typical scope

  • Network and server monitoring
  • SNMP / Syslog / NetFlow
  • Cloud and application telemetry
  • Uptime / service checks
  • Capacity and performance trends
  • Digital experience monitoring when included in the engagement
  • Alert normalization and routing
  • Ticket / escalation integration
  • Dashboards and operational review

Common reasons for the work

  • Alerts exist but are noisy or ignored
  • Capacity problems are discovered too late
  • Multiple tools show different pieces of the outage
  • IT needs a clearer operational picture across sites

How we run the work

The work is planned around the environment that already exists. We verify what depends on what, make the change in a controlled way, and document the pieces that matter for support.

We do not intentionally create lock in by withholding routine knowledge, credentials or documentation. Customers receive the access and SOPs appropriate to the environment and the agreed support model.

Ways to engage

  • Assessment
  • Architecture / design
  • Migration / implementation
  • Troubleshooting
  • Managed / co-managed operations

Customer control

Use us as much or as little as you need.

Some customers want Orbiwest to run the environment. Others want their own team to stay hands on. We support both models. The customer keeps ownership, visibility and the ability to operate independently.

Customer owned accountsCore business accounts, tenants and systems remain under customer ownership.
Shared visibilityAuthorized contacts can see the systems, documentation and service information relevant to their environment.
SOPs for common tasksWe document repeatable tasks so your staff can handle the work they want to own.
No unnecessary dependencyWe do not intentionally hide credentials, documentation or routine knowledge to create lock in.
You choose the operating model. If you want Orbiwest to fully manage the environment, we can take that responsibility. If you want your internal team to operate it, we provide access, documentation and SOPs so they can do that confidently. Co managed models can sit anywhere between those two points.

Need help across more than one technology?

That is normal. Most real environments cross identity, endpoints, network, cloud, security, applications and physical infrastructure. Bring us the environment, not a perfectly categorized ticket.
