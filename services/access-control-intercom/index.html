Service

Access Control, Credentials & Intercom

Door access, credentials, intercom and entry-system integration coordinated with the surrounding network and IT environment.

When this helps

Common reasons organizations call us

  • Cards or key fobs do not enroll or unlock reliably
  • Door schedules and access levels are confusing
  • Intercom and camera systems are separate
  • Controllers/readers have network connectivity issues
  • Credential offboarding is inconsistent
  • A site needs to modernize entry systems without replacing everything at once

What the service can include

  • Controller and reader configuration when included in the engagement
  • RFID card and key-fob enrollment
  • PIN/mobile credential support where available
  • Access levels, door groups and schedules
  • Intercom/video-entry integration
  • Camera association with door events where supported
  • Network connectivity and segmentation
  • System troubleshooting and modernization
  • Administrative documentation and handoff

How Orbiwest approaches the work

We keep the technical work tied to the real problem. That means checking the surrounding systems, planning the change and leaving enough documentation for future support.

What we document

Material configurations, decisions, dependencies and handoff information are documented as appropriate to the engagement so future support does not depend only on memory.

Where this helps most

  • Clear technical ownership
  • Practical troubleshooting
  • Maintainable configuration
  • Better documentation
  • A realistic next step
