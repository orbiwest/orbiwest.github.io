Service

Cameras, NVR/VMS & AI Analytics

IP cameras, recording, storage, remote viewing, camera networking and supported AI video analytics for modern business environments.

When this helps

Common reasons organizations call us

  • Cameras are offline or unreliable
  • NVR/VMS storage or retention is insufficient
  • Remote viewing is difficult or insecure
  • Camera networks share business traffic without planning
  • Recorded events are difficult to find
  • The organization wants useful AI analytics without unnecessary complexity

What the service can include

  • IP/PoE camera planning and configuration
  • NVR/VMS administration
  • Recording schedules and retention planning
  • Remote/mobile viewing
  • Camera VLAN and PoE network design
  • Camera health, firmware and hardening
  • Person/vehicle and smart-event analytics where supported
  • Smart search / metadata workflows
  • Multi-site viewing by platform capability
  • Existing-system modernization

Make recording and retrieval reliable first

Coverage, camera health, PoE/networking, recorder capacity, retention and secure viewing come before optional analytics. AI features are useful only when the base system records reliably.

What we document

Camera/recorder inventory, addressing/network placement, recording/retention settings, viewing access and meaningful analytics configuration are documented where supported.

Good fit when you need

  • Clear technical ownership
  • Practical troubleshooting
  • Maintainable configuration
  • Better documentation
  • A realistic next step
