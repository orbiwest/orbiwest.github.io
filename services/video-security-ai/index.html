Cameras, NVR, Cameras & AI Video

IP camera, NVR/VMS, remote viewing, recording, storage, network design, maintenance and AI video analytics for business, education and multi-site environments.

Cameras, Access Control + IT

Cameras are network systems now.

Modern video security combines cameras, PoE switching, VLANs, storage, NVR/VMS software, remote access, cybersecurity and increasingly edge or server-side AI. Orbiwest approaches the camera system as part of the technology environment—not as an isolated box on the wall.

AI capabilities vary by camera, recorder, VMS, firmware, licensing and local requirements. Orbiwest scopes analytics and integrations around the actual platform and use case rather than promising features every camera cannot support.

Common integrations

  • Access control and door-event video association
  • Intercom and entry systems
  • Alarm/event notifications
  • Network segmentation and PoE infrastructure
  • Cloud, edge and on-premises video-management architectures
  • Multi-site viewing and administration
  • Supported third-party VMS / ONVIF workflows

Privacy and security matter

Video systems contain sensitive operational data. Camera credentials, recorder access, remote viewing, retention, network exposure and AI use should be designed deliberately and reviewed as part of the overall security posture.

Typical project flow

  • Assess the current environment and coverage goals
  • Confirm camera/network/storage requirements
  • Design the recorder/VMS and access model
  • Implement, test and document
  • Train responsible users on viewing and retrieval
  • Plan maintenance and future expansion
