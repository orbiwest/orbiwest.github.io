Cameras, NVR/VMS & AI Analytics

IP cameras, recording, storage, remote viewing, camera networking and supported AI video analytics for modern business environments.

Reduce noise. Clarify risk. Know who acts when it matters.

When this helps

Where the risk usually shows up

  • Cameras are offline or unreliable
  • NVR/VMS storage or retention is insufficient
  • Remote viewing is difficult or insecure
  • Camera networks share business traffic without planning
  • Recorded events are difficult to find
  • The organization wants useful AI analytics without unnecessary complexity

Controls we can operate with you

  • IP/PoE camera planning and configuration
  • NVR/VMS administration
  • Recording schedules and retention planning
  • Remote/mobile viewing
  • Camera VLAN and PoE network design
  • Camera health, firmware and hardening
  • Person/vehicle and smart-event analytics where supported
  • Smart search / metadata workflows
  • Multi-site viewing by platform capability
  • Existing-system modernization

How we keep security ownership clear

The first job is to understand what the business is relying on. From there we can decide whether the right answer is a repair, cleanup, migration or replacement.

What we leave behind

Material configurations, decisions, dependencies and handoff information are documented as appropriate to the engagement so future support does not depend only on memory.

Security responsibilities are documented. The customer should know what is monitored, which actions Orbiwest can take, which actions require approval, and how to access the relevant reporting and tools.

Clients usually call us for

  • Clear technical ownership
  • Practical troubleshooting
  • Maintainable configuration
  • Better documentation
  • A realistic next step

Customer control

Security should increase control, not take it away.

You should know what is being monitored, what happens when an alert fires, who can make changes, and what authority Orbiwest has during an incident. We document those responsibilities instead of hiding them behind a security dashboard.
