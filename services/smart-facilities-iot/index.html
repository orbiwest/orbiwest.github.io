Environmental Sensors, IoT & Smart Facilities

Use connected sensors to watch the conditions around equipment, spaces and facilities—and route the events to people who can act on them.

What this covers

Practical scope

  • Server-room temperature and humidity monitoring
  • Water-leak sensors
  • Door / window contact sensors
  • Motion and glass-break sensor integration by platform
  • Panic-button integration by scope
  • Air-quality monitoring such as CO₂, PM2.5, TVOC and relative humidity where supported
  • Noise, ambient-light and occupancy sensing
  • Cold-storage / temperature-probe monitoring
  • Vape-detection integration where appropriate
  • Alerts to email, Teams, ticketing, dashboards or camera workflows

When it is worth looking at

  • Network closets can overheat without anyone noticing
  • A small leak could damage expensive equipment
  • Facilities teams want alerts tied to rooms and nearby cameras
  • Schools or offices need air-quality or environmental visibility
  • Sensor alerts exist but are not connected to an operational workflow

How we approach it

A sensor is only useful if somebody receives the right alert and knows what to do. We design the network placement, alert path, permissions and integration around the operational response—not around collecting more telemetry.
