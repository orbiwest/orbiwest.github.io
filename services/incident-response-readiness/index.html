Cybersecurity & Resilience

Incident Response & Recovery Readiness

Prepare the technical and communication decisions that become much harder when an outage, compromise or ransomware event is already underway.

What we can take on

Practical scope

  • Incident roles and escalation paths
  • Technical response runbooks
  • Identity and endpoint containment considerations
  • Backup and restore dependencies
  • Cyber-insurance and outside-counsel coordination points
  • Tabletop exercises when included in the engagement
  • Emergency contact and vendor documentation
  • Post-incident remediation planning

Signs it needs attention

  • Everyone assumes somebody else will know what to do
  • Cyber insurance has response requirements
  • Critical backup and recovery dependencies are undocumented
  • Leadership has never walked through a realistic incident scenario

What we pay attention to

We keep the plan operational. Who can disable an account? Who owns the firewall? Which backup should be restored first? Who contacts the insurer? Those decisions are more useful than a generic incident-response document nobody can execute.

Security responsibilities are documented. The customer should know what is monitored, which actions Orbiwest can take, which actions require approval, and how to access the relevant reporting and tools.

How we can engage

  • One-time assessment
  • Defined project
  • Co-managed support
  • Ongoing managed service
  • Vendor / platform coordination
