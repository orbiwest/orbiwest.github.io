FAQ

Frequently asked questions.

General questions about Orbiwest services and how engagements are scoped.

Do you provide both remote and on-site support?

Yes, depending on the agreed scope, location and type of work.

Do you work only with one firewall, cloud or endpoint vendor?

No. Recommendations depend on the environment, existing investments, supportability and business requirements.

Can you guarantee a specific response time?

Response commitments depend on the applicable agreement. This public website intentionally does not publish a blanket SLA.

Do you support small organizations?

Yes. Scope is based on the environment and needs, not just organization size.
