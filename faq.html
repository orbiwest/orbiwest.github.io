FAQ

Common questions about Orbiwest.

A concise overview of services, positioning and support communication.

What does Orbiwest Technologies do?

Orbiwest provides managed IT, end-user support, network engineering, wireless, cybersecurity, cloud, server administration, backup-readiness, projects and onsite IT support.

Where is Orbiwest based?

Orbiwest Technologies LLC is based in Chicago, Illinois, USA. Public website content intentionally does not expose a residential registered-agent address.

Do you publish customer names or testimonials?

Not without permission. The representative-work pages are intentionally anonymized and do not present fabricated testimonials or measured results.

Can you support remote users?

Many endpoint, cloud, identity and troubleshooting tasks can be handled remotely. Some physical infrastructure and device issues require onsite support.

Do you guarantee compliance?

No. Orbiwest can help implement practical security and operational controls, but legal or regulatory compliance requires environment-specific review and appropriate professional advice.

How do I request support?

Existing support communication can be sent to support@orbiwest.com. New business inquiries can be sent to info@orbiwest.com.
