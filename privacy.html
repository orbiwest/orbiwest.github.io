Privacy

Website privacy notice.

This notice explains the basic handling of information when using the Orbiwest Technologies website.

Information you provide

If you email Orbiwest Technologies, the information in your message is received through the relevant email systems. The static GitHub version of the website does not submit the contact form to a server; it creates a draft in the visitor's email client.

Website hosting

The website may be hosted through GitHub Pages and/or Cloudflare. Those providers may process standard technical request data needed to deliver and secure the website.

Analytics and cookies

This package does not include advertising pixels or third-party analytics by default. If analytics, embedded forms, chat, advertising or additional tracking are added later, this notice should be reviewed before deployment.

Contact

Privacy questions may be sent to info@orbiwest.com.
