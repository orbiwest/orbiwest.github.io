Privacy

Privacy policy for website visitors.

This notice explains the basic information practices associated with the Orbiwest Technologies website and email enquiries.

Effective date

August 14, 2026

This policy applies to the public website at orbiwest.com. It may be updated as the website and services change.

Information you provide

If you contact Orbiwest Technologies by email, we receive the information you choose to send, which may include your name, email address, organization, contact details, technical information and message content.

Technical information

Hosting, network and security providers may process standard technical request information needed to deliver and protect the website, such as IP address, browser or device information, requested pages, timestamps and security logs.

Cookies and analytics

The current website does not intentionally include a first-party advertising or behavioral tracking system. If analytics, advertising, forms or other tracking technologies are added later, this notice should be updated to describe those practices.

How information may be used

Information may be used to respond to enquiries, provide requested information, evaluate service requests, maintain website and system security, keep business records and comply with applicable legal obligations.

Service providers and disclosures

Information may be processed by service providers that support website hosting, email, security or business operations when reasonably necessary for those functions. Information may also be disclosed when required by law or to protect legitimate legal or security interests.

Retention

Information is retained only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purpose for which it was received, business recordkeeping, security, dispute resolution or applicable legal requirements.

External links

This website may link to third-party websites. Their privacy practices are governed by their own policies and are not controlled by Orbiwest Technologies.

Contact

Questions about this privacy notice may be sent to info@orbiwest.com.
