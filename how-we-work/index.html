How Orbiwest works

Customer ownership comes first.

We can manage the entire environment, work beside your team, or hand the operation back after a project. The customer should understand the systems, retain the agreed access and receive useful documentation in every model.

We explain the work

Before an important change, we explain what is being changed, why it is needed, what can be affected and how the result will be verified. Afterward, we tell you what changed and what we learned. The goal is not to overwhelm customers with jargon. It is to make the environment understandable enough that decisions are informed.

You keep ownership and access

Customer owned tenants, domains, infrastructure, data and business systems remain customer owned. Authorized customer contacts receive the agreed administrative access. High privilege access is still protected with MFA, least privilege, separate administrator roles and break glass procedures where appropriate.

We document the environment

Documentation should help the next person support the system. Depending on the service, that can include network diagrams, addressing, VLANs, firewall changes, cloud and identity dependencies, server and storage information, backup and recovery procedures, security escalation rules, integrations, device inventories and configuration handoff notes.

We provide SOPs

If a task is repeatable and appropriate for the customer to perform, we can document it as a standard operating procedure. Examples include adding or disabling users, assigning licenses, enrolling cards or key fobs, checking backups, reviewing alerts, performing common recovery steps, or making routine administrative changes.

You decide how much we manage

  • Fully managed: Orbiwest handles the agreed operational work and remains the primary technical owner.
  • Co managed: Your IT team and Orbiwest divide responsibility based on skills, availability and business needs.
  • Project and handoff: We assess, design, implement and document the change, then your team operates it.
  • Advisory: Your team performs the work while Orbiwest provides design, review, escalation or specialist guidance.

We do not use documentation as leverage

We do not want a customer to stay with Orbiwest because leaving would be confusing or risky. We want the relationship to continue because the service is useful. That means keeping ownership, access and documentation clean enough that the customer has a real choice.

Customer access is provided through a secure operating model. Direct unrestricted administrator access is not appropriate for every user or every system. We use designated authorized contacts, least privilege, MFA and documented emergency access where appropriate.
Customer control

You stay informed and in control.

Orbiwest is here to make technology easier to operate, not to make the customer dependent on us. We explain what we are changing, keep ownership clear, and leave useful documentation behind.

Want Orbiwest to manage everything?

We can. Want your team to stay hands on? We can support that too. The operating model should fit the customer, not the provider.
