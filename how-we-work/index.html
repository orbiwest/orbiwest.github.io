How We Work

Understand first. Improve with purpose.

A disciplined technical process keeps changes tied to business needs and makes systems easier to operate after the work is complete.

01

Understand

Review users, systems, dependencies, ownership, risks and pain points.

02

Stabilize

Address urgent reliability, access, configuration and security issues first.

03

Standardize

Improve documentation, configuration consistency and supportability.

04

Improve

Prioritize sensible upgrades that reduce recurring problems and risk.

Operating Principles

Clear work. Clear ownership.

Technical work should leave the environment easier to understand and support.

  • Change only what needs to change
  • Document important configuration and decisions
  • Keep rollback and recovery in mind
  • Communicate impact in plain language
  • Avoid unnecessary complexity
