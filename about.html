About

Orbiwest Technologies

A Chicago-based technology services company focused on dependable infrastructure, security-minded operations and clear technical ownership.

Purpose

Technology should support the work.

Orbiwest is built around the idea that organizations should be able to rely on technology without being buried in unnecessary complexity.

That means understanding the environment, fixing root causes where practical, documenting important decisions and treating security as part of normal operations—not as an afterthought.

What good looks like

  • Clear ownership of issues and changes
  • Configurations that can be maintained
  • Practical documentation
  • Security controls teams can actually operate
  • Technology decisions tied to business needs
