About

Technology should support the mission—not become the mission.

Orbiwest Technologies LLC is a Chicago-based technology services company focused on practical engineering, dependable support and security-minded operations.

Our approach

Clear thinking. Careful execution.

Orbiwest is built around a simple principle: organizations should be able to rely on their technology without being buried in unnecessary complexity.

That means documenting systems, communicating clearly, fixing root causes where possible, and treating security as part of normal operations rather than an afterthought.
