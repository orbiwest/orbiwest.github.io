# Orbiwest Customer Control Principles

1. Customer owned systems remain customer owned.
2. Authorized customer contacts receive the agreed access to customer owned systems.
3. Privileged access is still protected with MFA, least privilege and controlled administrator roles.
4. Important work is explained before changes are made.
5. Material changes, dependencies and handoff information are documented.
6. SOPs are provided for repeatable customer operated tasks when appropriate.
7. Customers may choose fully managed, co managed, project handoff or advisory service models.
8. Orbiwest does not intentionally create lock in by withholding routine credentials, documentation or operational knowledge.
9. Security response authority and escalation responsibilities are agreed explicitly.
10. The goal is to make customers more confident and capable, whether Orbiwest remains fully involved or not.
