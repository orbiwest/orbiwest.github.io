FAQ

Frequently asked questions.

General questions about Orbiwest services, scope and how engagements are handled.

Do you provide both remote and on-site support?

Yes, depending on scope, location and type of work. Infrastructure changes and physical troubleshooting may require on-site support.

Can you work alongside internal IT or another provider?

Yes. Orbiwest can support a defined technical area, assist with projects or handle work the existing team does not have time to complete.

Do you only support one firewall or cloud platform?

No. Recommendations depend on the environment, existing investments, supportability and business requirements.

Can you assess the environment before ongoing support begins?

Yes. An assessment can review documentation, accounts, endpoint maintenance, network configuration, backup questions and recurring issues before ongoing support is defined.

Can you help with a one-time project?

Yes. Defined projects such as firewall replacement, network cleanup, migrations, wireless work or server changes can be scoped separately.

Do you publish a blanket SLA on the website?

No. Response commitments should be defined in the applicable agreement rather than advertised without context.

Tell us what keeps breaking, what needs to improve, or what project you are planning.
