Backup Readiness Guide

A successful backup job is not the same as a proven recovery capability.

On this pageCoverageRetentionOffsite separationRestore testingRunbook

Coverage

Know which systems, SaaS data, endpoints and configurations are actually protected.

Retention

Confirm how long recoverable versions are retained and whether retention matches business needs.

Offsite separation

Avoid placing every copy inside the same failure domain.

Restore testing

Test representative recovery rather than relying only on green dashboard status.

Runbook

Document who initiates recovery, required credentials, vendor contacts and recovery priorities.

This guide is general operational guidance, not legal, compliance or vendor-specific professional advice. Adapt controls to your environment and requirements.
