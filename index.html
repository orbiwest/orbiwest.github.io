How we work

Understand. Plan. Change. Verify. Document.

Technical work should leave the environment clearer than we found it.

1UnderstandWhat is happening, who depends on it, what cannot go down and what success looks like.
2PlanMap dependencies, define the change, set the window and decide how to roll back if needed.
3ChangeMake controlled changes with the right engineer, the right access and the right communication.
4VerifyTest the real outcome, not just whether a console shows green.
5DocumentLeave behind the information, SOPs and ownership needed for confident support.
