How we work

A disciplined operating model.

Good IT work is less about flashy tooling and more about understanding the environment, reducing risk, documenting decisions and leaving systems easier to support.

01

Understand

Review the environment, business impact, dependencies and current pain points.

02

Stabilize

Address urgent reliability, access, security and configuration issues first.

03

Standardize

04

Improve
