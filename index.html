Frequently Asked Questions

Clear answers before the work starts.

Do you provide both remote and on-site support?

Yes, depending on the agreed scope, location and type of work. Some issues are best handled remotely; infrastructure changes and physical troubleshooting may require on-site support.

Can you work alongside an internal IT person or another provider?

Yes. Orbiwest can support a defined technical area, assist with projects or handle work that the existing team does not have time to complete.

Do you only support one firewall or cloud platform?

No. Recommendations depend on the environment, existing investments, supportability and requirements.

Can you help clean up an environment before ongoing support begins?

Yes. An assessment can review documentation, accounts, endpoint maintenance, network configuration, backup questions and recurring issues before ongoing support is defined.

Do you publish a blanket response-time guarantee?

No. Response commitments should be defined in the applicable agreement or project scope rather than advertised without context.
