Frequently Asked Questions

Clear answers before the work starts.

Do you provide both remote and on-site support?

Yes, depending on the agreed scope, location and type of work. Some issues are best handled remotely; network changes, installations and physical troubleshooting may require on-site work.

Do you support organizations that already have some internal IT?

Yes. Orbiwest can support a specific technical area, help with projects, assist with overflow work, or provide operational support alongside an existing team.

Do you only work with one firewall or cloud vendor?

No. Recommendations depend on the environment, existing investments, supportability and requirements. Technical experience spans multiple common business platforms.

Can you guarantee a specific response time from the website?

No blanket response-time promise is published here. Response expectations should be defined in the applicable service agreement or project scope.

Can you help clean up an environment before taking over support?

Yes. An initial assessment can identify documentation gaps, account issues, patching gaps, network risks, backup questions and recurring support problems before ongoing support begins.
