Education Device Readiness

A practical checklist for shared and student device environments.

Inventory

Know device ownership, serials, assigned users or rooms, and support status.

Updates

Plan OS and application updates around testing and classroom schedules.

Access

Confirm student/staff policies, filtering, Wi-Fi and account access before high-stakes testing.

Spares

Maintain a realistic pool of chargers, adapters and replacement devices for common failures.

This resource is general operational guidance, not a substitute for environment-specific assessment or contractual requirements.
