## Trust rules used throughout
The website intentionally does NOT invent:
- customer counts
- years-in-business claims
- response-time guarantees
- uptime percentages
- testimonials
- awards
- vendor partnership status
- certifications
- client logos

Those should only be added when Orbiwest has real, verifiable material to support them.
