# Orbiwest Website Content Map

## Primary positioning
Orbiwest Technologies is a full-service MSP, MSSP and technology engineering provider. The company supports day-to-day IT operations, managed security, cloud modernization, network and infrastructure engineering, business continuity, AI/data/automation, business communications, smart infrastructure, and strategic projects.

## Canonical service taxonomy
1. Managed IT & Operations
2. Cybersecurity & Managed Security
3. Cloud & Modern Workplace
4. Network & Infrastructure
5. Servers, Backup & Business Continuity
6. AI, Data & Automation
7. Business Applications & Communications
8. Smart Infrastructure & Physical Security
9. Strategy, Projects & Procurement

## Content roles
- Homepage: positioning and navigation, not a complete catalog.
- Services: technical capability directory.
- Solutions: outcome-based paths that combine services.
- Industries: operating context and industry-specific priorities.
- Experience: representative team/platform coverage.
- Resources: educational guidance and glossary.
- How We Work: customer ownership, access, documentation, SOPs and engagement models.
- Contact: simple intake without requiring a technical diagnosis.

## Trust policy
Do not invent customer counts, years in business, response guarantees, 24/7 staffing, certifications, testimonials, partner status or client logos. Vendor/platform names indicate representative capability only unless a credential or partnership is explicitly verified.
