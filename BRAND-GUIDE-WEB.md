# Orbiwest web brand guide

Colors:
- Metallic Navy `#0B2E5F`
- Deep Navy `#061A33`
- Royal Blue `#165EAD`
- Brushed Silver `#C7CDD3`
- Champagne Gold `#C9A24B`
- Graphite `#4D545C`

Logo:
- Keep the eagle/globe emblem fully visible.
- Never crop the orbital ring.
- Use the emblem alone at tiny sizes.
- Use emblem + live text wordmark at normal header sizes.
- Do not place the logo inside a visible square or framed box.

Metallic finish:
- Keep it restrained.
- Metallic texture belongs mainly to the emblem and wordmark.
- UI cards, buttons and body text stay clean and mostly flat.

Primary positioning:
`Managed IT · Networks · Cybersecurity · Cloud`

Brand promise:
`Secure · Connect · Empower`

Homepage headline:
`Technology you can rely on. People you can reach.`
