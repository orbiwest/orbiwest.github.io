Network Documentation Checklist

What to record so troubleshooting and change work are less dependent on memory.

Addressing

Record subnets, gateways, DHCP scopes, static reservations and important network services.

Segmentation

Document VLAN IDs, purpose, tagging expectations and inter-VLAN policy.

Infrastructure

Record switches, firewalls, access points, uplinks, management addresses and support status.

Access

Store credential-handling procedures securely—not in public documentation.

General operational guidance only; environment-specific requirements may differ.
