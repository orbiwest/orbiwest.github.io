Network Documentation

What to record so troubleshooting and change work depend less on memory.

Use this guide as a practical review checklist. Exact controls, sequencing and responsibilities depend on the environment and service agreement.

Topology and uplinks

Document the firewall, switches, access points, important uplinks and how sites or closets connect. A simple accurate diagram is more useful than an elaborate outdated one.

Addressing and DHCP

Record subnets, gateways, DHCP scopes, exclusions, reservations, DNS sources and important static addresses.

Segmentation

Record VLAN IDs, names, purpose, tagging expectations and security policy between user, guest, voice, server, camera and management networks.

WAN, VPN and remote access

Document ISP handoffs, public addressing, site-to-site VPN dependencies, remote-access methods and failover behavior where applicable.

Device management

Record management IPs, model/serial information, firmware families, configuration-backup location and the approved administrative access method.

Keep secrets separate

Documentation should explain how access is obtained without embedding passwords, private keys or recovery codes in broadly shared documents.

Related service: Networks & Wireless. This guide is general operational information, not a substitute for environment-specific engineering, legal advice or compliance advice.
