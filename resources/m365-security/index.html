Microsoft 365 Security

Identity, MFA, administrator access and tenant hygiene to review regularly.

Use this guide as a practical review checklist. Exact controls, sequencing and responsibilities depend on the environment and service agreement.

MFA and sign-in controls

Protect user and privileged accounts with strong authentication. Review Conditional Access or equivalent controls where licensing and requirements support them.

Privileged roles

Keep administrator access limited to people who need it and separate high-privilege activity from ordinary email and web use where practical.

Stale users and guests

Remove or disable access that is no longer needed and review guest/external users that accumulated over time.

Third-party applications

Review OAuth/application consent and integrations that can read mail, files, contacts or tenant data.

Mail and sharing

Review forwarding, suspicious inbox rules, external sharing defaults and collaboration settings that affect business data.

Recovery and evidence

Know how deleted data, compromised accounts and administrator changes would be investigated or recovered. Native retention and backup expectations should be understood rather than assumed.

Related service: Microsoft 365 & Cloud. This guide is general operational information, not a substitute for environment-specific engineering, legal advice or compliance advice.
