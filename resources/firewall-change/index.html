Firewall Change Planning

Reduce avoidable downtime before a firewall migration or major policy change.

Use this guide as a practical review checklist. Exact controls, sequencing and responsibilities depend on the environment and service agreement.

Capture the current state

Export the configuration and record interface mappings, WAN information, routes, NAT, DHCP, VPNs, objects and non-obvious dependencies.

Define the rollback

Know what would cause a rollback, how the old path will be restored and how management access will be retained if the primary change fails.

Map dependencies

Identify VLANs, DNS/DHCP sources, site-to-site tunnels, remote access, hosted services, voice systems and applications that depend on policy.

Control the cutover

Use a maintenance window appropriate to the risk, limit unrelated changes and keep a validation checklist visible during the work.

Validate deliberately

Test internet access, routing, DNS, DHCP, VPNs, remote management, critical applications, logging and published services after the change.

Document what changed

Record final configuration, deviations from plan, outstanding follow-up and the location of configuration backups.

Related service: Firewalls, VPN & Secure Connectivity. This guide is general operational information, not a substitute for environment-specific engineering, legal advice or compliance advice.
