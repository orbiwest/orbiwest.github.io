Firewall Change Planning

Reduce avoidable downtime before a firewall migration or major policy change.

Before the change

Export configuration, document current state, confirm management access and define rollback.

Dependencies

Review WAN handoff, VLANs, DHCP, routes, NAT, VPNs and critical applications.

During the change

Change only what is required and keep management access available where possible.

After the change

Validate internet, routing, VPNs, DNS, DHCP, applications and logging.

General operational guidance only; environment-specific requirements may differ.
