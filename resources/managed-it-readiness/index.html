Managed IT Readiness

What to clarify before outsourcing or restructuring day-to-day IT support.

Know the environment

Document users, endpoints, servers, network devices, cloud tenants, vendors and business-critical applications.

Define ownership

Clarify who owns account administration, vendor escalation, purchasing, backups and security decisions.

Identify recurring pain

List the issues that consume the most time or create the most disruption.

Set realistic expectations

Agree on scope, access, escalation and what is explicitly out of scope.

General operational guidance only; environment-specific requirements may differ.
