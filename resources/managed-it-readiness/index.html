Managed IT Readiness

What to clarify before outsourcing or restructuring day-to-day IT support.

Use this guide as a practical review checklist. Exact controls, sequencing and responsibilities depend on the environment and service agreement.

1. Inventory the environment

List users, endpoints, servers, network devices, cloud tenants, business applications, vendors and locations. The goal is not perfect documentation; it is knowing what must be supported.

2. Define ownership

Decide who owns user administration, vendor escalation, hardware purchasing, backups, security decisions, renewals and major changes. Ambiguous ownership becomes a recurring support problem.

3. Identify recurring pain

Capture the issues that consume staff time: access problems, unstable Wi-Fi, aging devices, vendor handoffs, patching, backups, printers or recurring application issues.

4. Prepare secure access

Know how administrative access will be delegated, how MFA will work and how credentials or secrets will be exchanged. Avoid sending passwords or recovery codes through ordinary email.

5. Agree on scope and escalation

Clarify what is included, what requires a project, which systems are business-critical, who can approve changes and how urgent issues are escalated.

6. Plan onboarding and documentation

Expect discovery, documentation, tool deployment, access verification and confirmation of responsibilities before steady-state support is predictable.
