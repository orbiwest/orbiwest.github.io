Practical Security Baseline

A sensible starting point for identity, patching, endpoint protection and recovery.

Identity

Use MFA where supported, remove stale accounts and minimize unnecessary administrator rights.

Patching

Keep operating systems and commonly exploited applications on a defined update cadence.

Endpoint protection

Use supported endpoint protection and make sure alerts have a clear owner.

Recovery

Know what is backed up, who owns restoration and how important systems would be rebuilt.

General operational guidance only; environment-specific requirements may differ.
