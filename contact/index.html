You do not need to arrive with a diagnosis. Tell us what is happening, who is affected, what changed recently and what a good outcome would look like. We can take it from there.

Email info@orbiwest.com.

Helpful information to include

  • What is happening or what you want to improve
  • How many users, devices or locations are affected
  • Whether this is ongoing support or a specific project
  • Any important deadline or maintenance window
  • Current vendors/platforms if they are relevant

What happens after you contact us

We start by understanding the problem and what you want to own internally. Before important work begins, we make the responsibilities clear: what Orbiwest will manage, what your team will manage, what access each side needs, and what documentation or SOPs should be delivered.

Customer control

You stay informed and in control.

Orbiwest is here to make technology easier to operate, not to make the customer dependent on us. We explain what we are changing, keep ownership clear, and leave useful documentation behind.

Explain the workWe tell you what is changing, why it is needed, and what the impact will be.
Keep customer accessAuthorized customer contacts retain the agreed access to customer owned systems.
Document and hand offImportant decisions, configurations and dependencies are documented.
Provide SOPsRoutine tasks are written down so your team can operate confidently when you want to.
You choose the operating model. If you want Orbiwest to fully manage the environment, we can take that responsibility. If you want your internal team to operate it, we provide access, documentation and SOPs so they can do that confidently. Co managed models can sit anywhere between those two points.
